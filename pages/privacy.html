2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it:

  • Email address — when you subscribe to our mailing list, download a free product, or submit the contact form
  • Name — if provided during sign-up or contact
  • Message content — when you use the contact form
  • Payment information — processed by Gumroad; we do not store card details

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver products you have requested (books, digital downloads)
  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form
  • To send you updates about new releases, products, and content, if you opted in
  • To improve our website and services

We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Legal Basis (POPIA)

We process your information on the basis of your consent (opt-in) and to fulfil a contract with you (delivery of purchased or free products, or responding to a request you initiated).

5. Data Retention

We retain your email address until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Contact form submissions are retained for as long as needed to resolve the enquiry. You may request deletion at any time by emailing dfnworldwide@gmail.com.

6. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services may process your data as part of how this website actually operates:

  • Formspree — form submission handling
  • n8n — automation that routes form submissions to our CRM and email tools
  • Google Sheets — internal CRM record-keeping
  • Brevo — email sequences for subscribers who opt in
  • Gumroad — product delivery and payment processing (gumroad.com/privacy)
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting (privacy.github.com)

7. Your Rights (POPIA)

You have the right to: access your personal information · correct inaccurate information · request deletion · withdraw consent at any time · lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

8. Cookies

This website may use essential cookies and browser storage for functionality (for example, remembering that you've dismissed the countdown banner, or your progress in the Discipline Tracker, which stays on your device). No advertising or tracking cookies are used without your consent.
